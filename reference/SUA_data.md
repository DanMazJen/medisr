# Serum Uric Acid and Cardiovascular Disease Risk Factors

This dataset contains serum uric acid measurements, cardiovascular
disease risk factors, and basic demographic information for participants
in a retrospective cohort study conducted every two years from 2010 to
2018 in Hangzhou, Zhejiang Province, Southeastern China. A total of
6,119 participants aged 40 years and above who underwent at least three
physical examinations were enrolled.

## Usage

``` r
SUA_data
```

## Format

### `SUA_data`

A data frame with 6,119 rows and 15 columns:

- ID:

  Unique identifier for each participant

- Age:

  Age of the participant in years

- Sex:

  Sex of the participant. 1 = male; 2 = female

- BMI:

  Body mass index of the participant

- SBP:

  Systolic blood pressure of the participant

- DBP:

  Diastolic blood pressure of the participant

- FBG:

  Fasting blood glucose level of the participant

- TC:

  Total cholesterol level of the participant

- Cr:

  Serum creatinine level of the participant

- GFR:

  Glomerular filtration rate of the participant

- UA:

  Serum uric acid level in the participant's blood

- Times:

  Number of medical follow-up visits for the participant

- Hypertension:

  Hypertension status. 1 = no; 2 = yes

- Hyperglycemia:

  Hyperglycemia status. 1 = no; 2 = yes

- Dyslipidemia:

  Dyslipidemia status. 1 = no; 2 = yes
